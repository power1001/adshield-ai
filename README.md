## 🎯 The $100K/mo SaaS Opportunity

Every digital marketer, affiliate, e-commerce dropshipper, and agency running ads on Meta (Facebook & Instagram) or Google lives in constant fear of **Ad Account & Business Manager (BM) Bans**. 
- Automated AI ban bots evaluate ads in milliseconds.
- Trigger words like *"Are you in debt?"*, *"Melt belly fat"*, or *"Make $10K in 7 days"* result in immediate ad rejection, account quality penalties, or permanent BM suspension.
- An account ban halts business revenue and wastes thousands in unrecoverable pixel data.

**AdShield AI** acts as an automated compliance firewall:
1. **Pre-Flight Scans** ad copy, creative, and landing page URLs against 30+ official Meta Advertising Standards.
2. **Calculates Ban Probability Score (0 - 100%)** with exact policy reference breakdowns.
3. **1-Click AI "Safe Mode" Rewriter** neutralizes ban bot triggers while preserving aggressive conversion copy and high CTR.
